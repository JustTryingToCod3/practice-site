A Little Breathing Room

Small steps. Soft landings. No perfect answers required.

Whatever you're feeling, you can start right where you are. Get a coping skill, explore what might be going on, hear a little kindness, or talk through your next step. You don't have to figure everything out at once.

You can take this one step at a time.
